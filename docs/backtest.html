Backtest

Does it beat the teams?

For every class, the model only learns from the drafts before it, and its settings are tuned on those earlier drafts too. Then it ranks the new class, and I compare that with the order teams actually picked in.

What changed in V4

  • Honest numbers. V3's settings were picked on the same backtest it reported. Now every setting is tuned only on classes before the one being tested, so the numbers below are lower than V3's but they're real.
  • Fixed how busts are counted. V3 gave a pick who never played a 0 but a rotation player with a bad season a negative number, so never playing beat playing badly. Now a season below replacement counts as replacement. That alone took the draft order's Immediate correlation from 0.06 to 0.42, so V3's big Immediate win was mostly that bug.
  • Two per-possession targets that don't reward a pick for just getting minutes: Peak BPM, and my own plus-minus (RAPM) built from every NBA lineup since 2007-08.
  • A ridge regression blended with LightGBM, and college stats from the last three seasons instead of one, plus shot types (rim, mid range, dunks, assisted) from 2013-14 on.
  • Ranges that are checked: 80% ranges calibrated on earlier classes, and the share that held is shown below for every class.

What I tried and kept or dropped

Each idea against the version without it, averaged over all three targets' test classes. With 11 to 15 classes per target, a change smaller than about 0.03 in Spearman is noise, so an idea had to help on average and in most classes to stay.
